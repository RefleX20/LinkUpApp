01 Events feed

Every plan in one feed

Your groups' events, grouped by day and counted down to the minute. Open LinkUp and you already know what's next — today, tomorrow, and the rest of the week — with your RSVP on every card.

02 RSVPs

One tap, and everyone knows

Going, Maybe, or Can't Go. Answers update live for the whole group, with capacity limits, RSVP deadlines, and a Who's Going list — so nobody has to ask twice in the chat.

03 Group messaging

Sort the details in the group

A message thread for every group, with reactions, replies, and polls for picking a date. Tag the event you're talking about and everyone knows exactly which plan you mean.

04 Groups

A home for every group

Scan a QR code or type a six-character code and you're in. Each group gets its own space — events, chat, members, and admin controls for whoever's running it. Private groups stay approval-only.

Privacy

Private by default

Only what your group needs to see ever syncs to our servers — everything else stays on your phone. Here's how that splits:

Stays on your phone

Your personal calendar events and your sign-in credentials never leave your device — credentials live in the iOS Keychain, and personal events are never uploaded.

Syncs — protected

Group events, RSVPs, and messages sync so your group can see them. Event locations are stored encrypted on our servers.

Never sold, never personalized

Ads are non-personalized, and your data is never sold. The plain-language detail is in the Privacy Policy below.
